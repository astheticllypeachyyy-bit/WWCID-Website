    Personal Actions

    Make an eco-brick:



    Materials:


    • Plastic bottle (do not buy one, only use one that you naturally bought out of necessity)
    • A stick or something slightly smaller than the head of your bottle and long enough to reach to the bottom of the bottle
    • Any plastic trash that naturally accumulates


    Procedure:


    • Whenever you have trash, place it inside the plastic water bottle.
    • When you notice your bottle starting to fill up, use the stick to compact the trash.
    • Once you cannot compact the inside of the bottle anymore, your eco-brick is done. You can now throw it out, or keep it for another purpose; whatever is your preference.


    Safely pick up litter:



    Materials:


    • Trash bag (preferably a compostable one)
    • Something to pick the trash up with (eg. dustpan/trash picker, tongs)
    • Gloves


    Procedure:


    • Go out to a place where you know has litter and is safely accessible
    • Use your tool to pick up the trash you see in the area
    • You can document the process but this step is optional, it is up to you
    • Once your bag is full, most of the trash has been removed, or you want to stop, tie off the bag(s) and dispose of them how you would typically dispose of trash.
    • If possible try to separate the recyclables in a separate bag


    Compost (you get the bonus of free fertilizer):



    Materials:


    • Compost container (If you plan on placing this inside or near your home, make sure smell cannot easily escape the container or is masked)
    • A long stick or something to stir with (Should be bigger than the container to keep distance when mixing)
    • Any organic scraps that you have

    Procedure:


    • Add organic scraps to the compost bin
    • Add water to it every once in a while
    • Use the stick to stir the contents however often you see fit
    • The process may take a few weeks to see results when first starting out so be patient

    Make paper beads:


    Materials:

    • Left over paper
    • Glue
    • Scissors
    • Wooden dowel/toothpick
    • Nail polish/varnish

    Procedure:

    • Start by cutting your paper into long triangle strips, with one end of the triangle being the thickness you want for the bead
    • Wrap the thick end of paper around the dowel tightly so it does not slip off when continuing to the next steps
    • Along the way, periodically add glue so the paper does not unravel
    • Continue adding glue and wrapping the paper around the dowel
    • Glue down the thin end of the triangle and allow for the glue to dry
    • Add the nail polish/varnish to seal the bead off, be careful to not get too much on the edges or it may stick to the dowel/toothpick
